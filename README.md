### Features

- **Unlimited recording** — No time limits, no watermarks
- **Auto-zoom on clicks** — Automatically focuses where it matters
- **AI voiceover & voice cloning** — Generate narration with your own voice
- **AI Smart Cut & Auto-Redact** — Remove silences and sensitive info automatically
- **AI captions on-device** — Accurate subtitles, fully private
- **Click sounds & keystrokes** — Authentic key-down and key-up audio
- **Webcam PiP with background blur** — Picture-in-picture with studio quality
- **iOS device recording** — Record iPhone & iPad via USB
- **Device frames & touch visualization** — Professional device mockups
- **Scene overlays & transitions** — Polished, production-ready videos
- **Multi-track timeline** — Full-featured editor with trim, split, and zoom
- **Multi-platform export** — One-click export for every platform
- **SOP & tutorial export** — Step-by-step documentation from recordings
- **137 wallpapers & backgrounds** — Curated collection built in
- **4K 120fps ProRes, WebM & GIF** — Studio-quality output formats
- **100% on-device** — Nothing leaves your Mac

### System Requirements

- macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or later
- Apple Silicon or Intel processor
- 500 MB free disk space
